# V12v12 Dockside Crew and Shore Wait Guide

## Shore leave and crew sheet
1. Sail back into harbour, complete the dock settlement, and allow the active crew to go ashore for recovery.
2. Press `F1` to open **DOCKMASTER / CREW & SHORE SHEET**. Use `1` / `2` / `3` for **Aboard**, **Shore**, and **Hire**.
3. In **Hire**, choose **HIRE ABOARD** or **HIRE ASHORE**. Recruitment costs yen. New recruits aboard are placed apart from existing crew to reduce crowding.
4. In **Shore**, the roster shows health, fatigue, skill, and whether each sailor is **RECOVERING**, **READY**, or **KEEP ASHORE**. **BRING ABOARD** returns one sailor immediately while alongside. **KEEP ASHORE** retains a deckhand when the vessel departs; **AUTO-RETURN** clears that preference.

## Wait until rested
1. In the **Shore** tab, click **WAIT UNTIL RESTED**.
2. Simulation time accelerates to 6× while automatically returning sailors recover. Ready means at least 98% health, at most 10% fatigue, and at least 95% morale.
3. When all automatic-return sailors are ready, they are brought aboard and the previous time scale is restored. Sailors marked **KEEP ASHORE** stay ashore.
4. Click **CANCEL WAIT** to stop early. Setting a course also stops the wait, restores the previous time scale, and recalls only automatic-return crew.

The stored wireless operator resumes radio watch when brought aboard individually, unless another operator has already been assigned. The larger per-fish auction feature remains deferred to 12v15.
